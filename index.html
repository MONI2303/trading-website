What is Candle Range Theory (CRT)?

Candle Range Theory is a technical analysis method that focuses on the relationship between the open, high, low, and close prices of candlesticks. It helps traders identify high-probability setups by analyzing price ranges and patterns.

Key CRT Concepts

🔥 High Range (HR)

A candle with an unusually large range compared to recent candles. Often signals strong directional move.

❄️ Low Range (LR)

A candle with a small range, indicating consolidation and potential breakout setup.

📊 Inside Bar

A candle that is completely inside the previous candle's range. Strong setup for breakout trading.

⬆️ Outside Bar

A candle that extends beyond the previous candle's range in both directions. Shows volatility expansion.

Common CRT Setups

  1. HR + HR: Two high range candles - trending market, good for momentum trades
  2. LR + LR: Two low range candles - consolidation, watch for breakout
  3. HR + LR: Range contraction after expansion - potential reversal or consolidation
  4. Inside Bar: Perfect for tight stop loss placement and risk management
  5. Outside Bar: Strong breakout signal, good for trend continuation

Trading Tips

  • Always trade in the direction of the larger timeframe trend
  • Use inside bars for tight stop loss placement
  • Wait for confirmation before entering trades
  • Manage risk with proper position sizing
  • Combine CRT with support/resistance levels for better accuracy
